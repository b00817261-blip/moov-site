MOOV Assistant — demo page

This page stands in for moovlogistics.com. The chat widget in the bottom-right corner is the whole integration — one script tag.

Try asking it:
• “What services do you offer?”
• “Do you handle customs clearance?”
• “How much does it cost to ship a container?” → it asks for your email instead of quoting a price
• “Where is my shipment?”
• “Who is MOOV?”

Captured leads are stored in localStorage["moov-assistant-leads"] and POSTed to MoovChatbotConfig.leadEndpoint when configured.
